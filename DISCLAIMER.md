## Security limitations

No software can provide “absolute” protection against all attackers or misconfiguration. AegisGrid is designed to reduce risk through local access control, integrity checking, and fail-closed network lockdown on detected tamper. You must still harden the host OS, protect signing keys, rotate credentials, and maintain physical access controls.
